# 12-week workplan

Source: concept whitepaper v1, section "Our assessment and the critical questions".
Start date: to be confirmed at the founders' session. Owners are proposals.

| # | Critical question | How we answer it | Owner | Weeks | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which 5 brands would pay for a pilot? | Shortlist 30 brands; 20 to 30 interviews; landing page with indicative prices | Aurelien, VJ | 1 to 4 | Not started |
| 2 | What one thing would a brand buy on its own? | Rank pain points from interviews; test market entry, ads and full operations as offers | Aurelien | 2 to 5 | Not started |
| 3 | Build our own agents or orchestrate existing tools? (VJ proposes widening to: which ERP do we build the harness on?) | Map each task to available tools and APIs; prototype the top 3 tasks; shortlist ERP platforms | Julien | 2 to 6 | Not started |
| 4 | Do we take a performance fee and accountability for results? | Model pricing and liability scenarios; test them in pilot contracts | VJ | 3 to 6 | Not started |
| 5 | How much really runs without people? | Run 3 to 5 paid pilots concierge-style; log every human intervention | All | 5 to 12 | Not started |
| 6 | Is consumer health and beauty the right start? | Compare demand and compliance effort by category | VJ, Aurelien | 4 to 10 | Not started |
| 7 | Who goes full-time, who funds, and are employer conflicts cleared? | Agree commitments, equity, IP and funding; check permissions with current employers | All | 1 to 3 | Not started |
