# 02-discovery

The 12-week workplan from the whitepaper. `workplan.md` tracks the seven
critical questions, owners and status.

- `brand-shortlist/`: the 30-brand shortlist and the interview guide (Q1).
- `interviews/`: one note per interview, `YYYY-MM-DD-<brand>.md` (Q1, Q2).
- `category-analysis/`: demand versus compliance effort by category (Q6).
- `pilots/`: one folder per paid pilot, including the human-intervention log (Q5).
