# Brand shortlist (Q1)

The 30-brand shortlist, the interview guide, and the landing page copy with
indicative prices. Target profile: mid-market and small branded CPG players,
roughly $500k to $1bn revenue, starting with consumer health and beauty.
Segments from the paper: non-EU brands entering Europe, European brands stuck
in one market, corporates testing a new country, founder-sellers stepping back.
